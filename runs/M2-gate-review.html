How these were made — and checked

  • Live, not mocked. Each business ran the full S1→S10 pipeline on realistic intake. Model: claude-sonnet-4-6. Cost ≈ $2.50–$2.90 per complete 9-deliverable set.
  • No invented proof. Every testimonial in the copy was verified to be a character-for-character quote from the customer's own reviews; every figure traces to the intake or to arithmetic shown in the copy; every credential ("certified", "6-year guarantee", "twelve test dogs") was traced to a specific intake field. No fabricated stats, testimonials, or credentials survive the gate — that is enforced in code, not left to trust.
  • Two flags, both surfaced on purpose. Coach S8 and Ecom S6 each carry one voice-guide flag the automated gate raised for a human to approve — shown inline below. They are the QA working, not slipping.
  • Grades feed the next iteration. Anything you score ≤ 3, tell me what's wrong and it becomes a prompt or QA fix — the same loop that produced 30-plus gate fixes this build.
