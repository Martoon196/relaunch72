Each plan retains its logical run identity, original owner authority, source/profile/editorial snapshot, query fingerprints, attempt nonces, known responses and model result. Before each new SERP request, the short brand→source/job transaction checks current authority, profile/knowledge, pause, editorial guidance and remaining monthly research allowance. It records one `search_calls` unit plus the provider-start nonce **before** calling the supplier. Failed attempts count too. This counter measures supplier attempts, not exact cash charges. Provider-reported costs are separate receipt evidence; historical or uncertain charges are never invented.

Only verified DataForSEO status **40101** is automatically retried: at most **two additional attempts across the whole logical run**, delayed 60 seconds then 300 seconds through persisted `runAfter`. The transport does not sleep or retry internally. Authentication, verification, payment, daily-cap, entitlement, other task errors and unknown HTTP/network outcomes receive allowlisted safe explanations and explicit recovery. Raw supplier `status_message` and response bodies are not shown.

Successful query receipts are reused after a known transient error. A known response whose database commit fails retries persistence with the same receipt/nonce, never the provider. Unknown started requests are held on worker reclaim. An exhausted, unknown or supplier-failed run persists a brand research hold so the next daily job cannot silently purchase the failure again. Owner resume uses a distinct authenticated command, current preflight and explicit acknowledgement; old history remains. Generic Retry cannot reset its run-wide supplier counters. A saved model result may be applied without another model call, subject to its original snapshot and current lease.

Optional keyword metrics have their own reserved attempt within the same existing allowance. No remaining headroom means no metrics call. Failure or an uncertain metrics outcome is a metrics-only warning: useful source research remains valid and demand stays qualitative. Metrics do not consume the two SERP retry slots and are never silently repeated. A genuine late receipt is retained without rewriting a plan that already used qualitative demand.

Purely pre-call profile/setup, pause, configuration or budget blockers keep an actionable blocked job but do not latch paid research when no call began. A later fresh daily intent may use corrected current owner controls. Once paid work began, uncertainty/failure/source changes retain explicit recovery. A previously claimed legacy job without durable receipts is held rather than purchased again. Only the latest unresolved legacy failure is imported into a safe generic hold; a newer success or stored explicit acknowledgement prevents historical re-holds. No old supplier status, cost or usage is inferred.
