# How to send a mentoring or Private payment link

For Martin. One page. Mentoring and One Lane Private are never linked on the website: each applicant gets their own link after an accepted call.

## Send one

1. Sign in at https://app.martinhowardproperty.co.uk/portal/login with your owner email.
2. Open **HQ → Applications** (https://app.martinhowardproperty.co.uk/hq/applications).
3. Find the applicant. In the **Payment link** column, choose the offer you agreed on the call:
   - One Lane Mentoring, founding price: £2,997 paid once, or 6 × £525 (£3,150). Only shown for applications received by Saturday 7 November 2026, 23:59 GMT.
   - One Lane Mentoring: £3,997 paid once, or 6 × £699 (£4,194).
   - One Lane Private: £25,000 paid once, or 12 × £2,250 (£27,000).
4. Press **Create single-use link**. The link appears under the applicant, marked "Not used yet".
5. Press **Email this link to …**. Your own email program opens with the applicant's address, a subject and a short message containing the link. Read it, edit if you like, and send it from your own account.

## What the applicant sees

Stripe's checkout with their email already filled in, the offer name, the full price and any instalment total, a required tick box agreeing to the terms (Private shows the Private terms), and the pay button. The link works for one payment only. After paying they land on a page saying you will be in touch within one working day to book their first session, they get Stripe's receipt and a welcome email with their sign-in link, and their access opens automatically.

## After they pay

The link shows **Paid**. Book their first session within one working day. Instalments stop by themselves after 6 or 12 payments.

## Change of plan

- Wrong offer, or they want to pay differently: press **Withdraw link** on the old one, then create the right one.
- Lost link: it is still shown under the applicant until it is used or withdrawn.

## Refunds

- Mentoring: 30 days from the first payment. The member can do it from their account page, or you can do it in Stripe.
- Private: full refund up to the first one-to-one session, then as the written terms say. Do it in the Stripe Dashboard for the MH196 Ltd account: for an instalment plan, **cancel the subscription first**, then refund each payment. Access moves to read-only automatically when Stripe confirms the refund.

## If payments are not connected

The column says "Payments are not connected yet". Grant access by hand instead: HQ → Access, enter the person's email, choose the product, add a note (for example "Paid by bank transfer, invoice 001"), and grant. That records access only, not a payment.
